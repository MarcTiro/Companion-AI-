Core Background

About the Project

Loneliness accelerates cognitive decline, impacts sleep quality, and increases mortality. Our research seeks to build culturally nuanced tools to identify and mitigate loneliness in urban populations.

Social Isolation Stats

Over 30% of young adults report persistent feelings of isolation in modern urban cities like Nairobi, driven by fragmented support structures and situationships.

Public Health Impact

Severe loneliness carries physical health risks equivalent to smoking 15 cigarettes a day, directly impacting sleep cycle regulations and heart health.

Current Research Gap

Existing diagnostic tools lack cultural grounding, ignoring local vernaculars, expressions of vulnerability, and typical urban code-switching practices.

AI-Driven Intervention

Providing real-time, low-friction, embedded AI agents offers immediate conversational support to vulnerable people when traditional services are unavailable.

Architecture Mapping

System Engineering

1

Data Layer

Inputs & Ingestion

Ingests records including textual logs with behavioral descriptions, localized speech transcripts, and social environment context metadata.

2

AI Layer

Feature & Models

Performs tokenization of code-switched text, extracts semantic embeddings, and uses fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa along with SVM baseline architectures.

3

Decision Layer

Clinical Inference

Classifies output into four clinically structured severity categories (Not Lonely, Borderline, Lonely, At Risk) while outputting calculated confidence scores.

4

Intervention Layer

Dynamic Dialogue

Triggers active chatbot sessions, pushes context-specific behavioral suggestions, and continuous wellbeing monitoring.

Academic Streams

Academic Streams

Research Question

How can we gather an ethically compliant, multi-dialect dataset representing expressions of loneliness across English, Kiswahili, and Sheng in urban settings?

Research Question

How does XLM-RoBERTa's cross-lingual capability optimize code-switched text classification compared to an SVM baseline model for detecting loneliness in behavioral descriptions?

Focus Area

Developing prompt structures and conversation nodes within Azure OpenAI to ensure dialogues are empathetic, culturally grounded, and safe.

Research Question

How can we prune and quantize XLM-RoBERTa and SVM models to run on mobile systems without degrading classification precision on behavioral text datasets?

Academic Timeline

Academic Roadmap

Stage 01

Foundation Literature

Reviewing sociological constructs of loneliness, public health metrics, and UCLA scale validation.

Builds the theoretical foundation for digital diagnostics in accordance with recognized scales.

Stage 02

Intermediate Studies

Investigating tokenization of code-switched text, XLM-RoBERTa properties, and SVM baseline performances.

Analyzes code-switched sheng, swahili, and english behavioral descriptions.

Stage 03

Advanced Implementations

Reviewing ONNX network optimization, conversational LLM architectures, and edge systems.

Resolves production deployment speed and embedded system runtime concerns.

Milestone Timeline

Semester 1

Literature Review & Setup

Conducting broad literature studies and establishing the core audio-text annotation schema.

Semester 2

Dataset Collection

Gathering Swahili, English, and Sheng loneliness phrases from volunteer cohorts in local contexts.

Semester 3

Model Development

Training and testing XLM-RoBERTa models and SVM baseline models on textual behavioral datasets.

Semester 4

Intervention Dialogue Engine

Semester 5

Deployment & Validation

Deploying compressed model ONNX runtimes onto mobile devices and running real-world trials.
